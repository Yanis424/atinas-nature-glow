Our Story

From the highlands,
to your skin.

Atinas was born in the Aurès mountains, where generations of Algerian women have pressed prickly pear seeds by hand and bathed in ghassoul clay. We work directly with these cooperatives, honoring ancestral methods while applying modern dermo-cosmetic rigor.

Every drop is traceable to its source. Every formula is cold-processed to preserve the active. Nothing is bleached, nothing is synthetic, nothing is tested on animals.
